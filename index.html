Generative world providers remain experimental adapters. Generated 3D/video carries geographic-registration quality and cannot silently become measurable GIS or operational truth.

THE SIMPLE PICTURE

You ask IQAI what you want. The Brain reads the real spatial state, chooses the right GIS/AI/simulation tools, runs them, puts the result back on the map, and keeps track of whether the result is observed, documented, calculated, inferred, simulated or AI-generated.
